World 02 / E2 · Save Point

We saved the past.
Did we save the future?

The first half of a route can create obligations for the second. Restoring completed actions does not necessarily preserve what the agent intended to do next.

6 models4 graphs5 strategies3,600 recoveries

One history. More than one future.

For each model, graph, and strategy, E2 captures three checkpoints after three or four completed calls and tries ten recoveries from each. The checkpoint histories match within that condition; they are not controlled to match across strategies.

Autopilot restores a saved executable plan. Other strategies can ask the model to decide unfinished actions again. A durable record of the past and a durable decision about the future serve different purposes.

A ticket survives the crash.

In our illustrative delivery story, walking back toward a different route leaves a ferry booking open. The destination is still reachable, but the world carries effects from the earlier choice. Continuing the booked route honors that earlier decision.

The experimental graph replay shows actual operations and crash resources. It does not assign invented business effects to anonymized calls.
